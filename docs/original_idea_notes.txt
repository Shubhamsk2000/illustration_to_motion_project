▸ Thought for 10s
  Yes, absolutely! You can build a 100% free pipeline and web app that turns a single still illustration (like mountains, a river, and a sun) into
  a subtle, living, looping animation.
                                                                                                                                                  
  Depending on the exact aesthetic and hardware you want to target (especially keeping in mind CPU-friendly setups), there are three proven       
  architectures:
  ──────
  ### Approach 1: Semantic Segmentation + Procedural Shaders (Recommended for Illustrations)
  
  Best for clean 2D/anime/illustration styles. Runs completely free, fast on CPU or in-browser WebGL, and produces seamless infinite loops without
  AI morphing/hallucinations.
  
    Single Image
       │
       ├──► 1. Segmentation (FastSAM / MobileSAM or Gemini Vision to mask: Water, Sky, Sun)
       │
       └──► 2. Element-Specific Motion Shaders:
               • River / Water ──► Directional UV flow map / wave ripple distortion
               • Sky / Clouds  ──► Slow directional drift & parallax offset
               • Sun / Light   ──► Subtle luminosity pulse & radial glow / rays
               • Trees / Foliage ─► Gentle sine-wave wind sway
       │
       └──► 3. Output: Seamless 5–10s Looping MP4 (via FFmpeg) or Live HTML5/WebGL Canvas
  
  • Pros: 100% free, runs on CPU or GPU, produces crisp art (zero blurring or glitching), loops perfectly.
  • Tech stack: Python + OpenCV/Pillow + FFmpeg (or Three.js/WebGL in the browser).
  ──────
  ### Approach 2: 2.5D Depth Parallax ("Live Wallpaper" Breathing Effect)
  
  Best for cinematic depth where the camera slowly orbits or sways, creating a 3D living window.
  
  1. Depth Estimation: Run Depth-Anything-V2 https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2 (the Small or Base model runs quickly even on a   
  standard CPU).
  2. Mesh Displacement: Project the image onto a 3D depth mesh.
  3. Camera Trajectory: Move a virtual camera in a gentle figure-8, slow zoom, or horizontal sway.
  4. Inpainting / Edge Stretching: Fill occluded borders.
  
  • Pros: Works automatically on any image with zero manual tagging or segmentation required.
  • Tech stack: depth-anything-v2 (PyTorch) + 3D warping via OpenCV or Three.js.
